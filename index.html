About

Built by someone who gets both worlds

I'm Mike Hughes. I live near Lahinch on the west coast of Ireland. I surf. I meditate. I've spent twenty years building enterprise software — and just as long sitting on a cushion.

By day, I lead national software delivery at Corlann, supporting over 6,000 staff in disability services. By early morning and evening, I build tools for the wellness world — because I believe this space deserves technology made with real care.

My wife is a somatic practitioner and meditation teacher. This isn't an abstract market to me. It's my life. Happy Techie exists because the people doing the most meaningful work often have the worst websites.
