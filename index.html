About Happy Techie

Good questions.
Interesting company.

Happy Techie is an evolving personal project bringing together technology, AI, personal development and a sense of adventure.

It’s about using technology, self-awareness and shared experiences to live happier, more meaningful lives.

The idea is simple: get together with curious people, explore interesting ideas, try practical techniques and tools, and have some fun along the way.

I’m Mike Hughes, exploring from the west coast of Ireland. You’re welcome to join the conversation.

Introducing the Happy Techie Lab

A little space to explore.

A relaxed, informal space for tech-minded people to connect, explore and experiment. Still taking shape — here are a few directions we could go.

01 / focus

The Inner Game

Awareness, attention, motivation and understanding ourselves. Get curious about what makes us tick.

02 / tinker

Tech & AI

Explore new tools and practical ways technology could improve our lives. Try things out and see what helps.

03 / venture

Side Quests

Personal challenges, small experiments and adventures. Follow an interest and enjoy where it takes you.

04 / connect

Lab Sessions

Small-group conversations, shared learning and encouragement. Bring a question, an idea or a little curiosity.

Early ideas, open possibilities. The Lab isn’t a scheduled programme — it’s something to shape together.
